Plus · The rules change

The contract will get you before the regulator does

Cyber Essentials now fails any applicant without multi-factor authentication on cloud services where it's available.

The Cyber Security and Resilience Bill brings medium and large IT providers into regulation, with 24-hour incident reporting.

Expect both in your customers' supplier questionnaires during 2027.

No big budget needed

Five things to do before January

  1. Switch on multi-factor authentication for every cloud service, starting with email.
  2. Agree a call-back rule for any change to bank details or urgent payment.
  3. Ask your IT provider four questions (they're in the report).
  4. List your five key suppliers, five key customers and the AI tools your staff use.
  5. Fill in the one-page plan and print it.
